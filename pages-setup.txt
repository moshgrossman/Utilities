SWITCHING ON THE UTILITIES WEB PAGE

One-time setup. Takes about 2 minutes. You only need a browser
signed in to GitHub.

When it is done, your tools live at:

https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/


1. Open github.com/moshgrossman/Utilities in Chrome.


2. Tap "Settings" — the tab with the gear, near the top of the
repo page. If you do not see it, widen the window, or tap the
"..." at the end of the tab row.


3. In the list on the left, tap "Pages".


4. Under "Build and deployment", check that "Source" says
"Deploy from a branch". If it does not, choose that.


5. Under "Branch", tap the dropdown that says "None" and choose
"main". Leave the folder next to it as "/ (root)". Tap "Save".


6. Wait about one minute, then refresh the page. You should see a
box at the top saying: "Your site is live at
https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/".

If it still shows nothing, wait another minute and refresh again —
the first build can be slow.


7. Tap "Visit site". You should see the dark Utilities page with
two buttons: the converter and the audio finder. Tap the converter
button and check it opens.


8. Optional but worth it: with the site open in Chrome, tap the
three-dot menu, then "Install app" (on some versions it says
"Add to Home screen"), then confirm. The tools open in their own
window from your home screen from now on — see open-as-app.txt
for the details.


IF THE SITE NEVER LOADS ON YOUR TABLET but works on another
device: that is the content filter not knowing github.io yet. Ask
for a permanent exception for moshgrossman.github.io — it is a
page of your own tools, nothing else, so it is an easy one to
describe. Until then, the downloaded-file way keeps working
exactly as before.


FROM NOW ON: any new tool added to the repo as an HTML file is
automatically on the site about a minute after it lands on main.
No re-setup needed.
