DAYCARE FORMS - HOW TO USE IT

Version 1.0


WHAT IT DOES

You type a child's details once. It gives you back every French form the office wants, already filled in, ready to print.

- The registration package: Entente pour garde BC, Formulaire de renseignements, Demande de contribution réduite, and the Fiche d'identification (in English, for the parents to fill in the health part by hand).

- Attendance sheets (Fiche d'assiduité): names and the two week dates. You write the P/A codes by hand.

- The Attestation des services de garde fournis, when a child leaves.

Everything prints on letter paper. Each form starts on a fresh sheet when you print double-sided.

Nothing is uploaded. Everything stays in Chrome on this tablet.


WHAT YOU NEED

Chrome, and the parents' details.

First time: about 3 minutes for your own daycare details.

Each new child: about 3 minutes.


STEP 1 - OPEN THE TOOL

Go to:

https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/daycare-forms/

You should see a dark page titled "Daycare Forms", with a yellow "+ New child" button.

If Chrome offers "Install app", say yes. It then opens like an app and works even with no internet.


STEP 2 - YOUR DAYCARE DETAILS (ONCE ONLY)

The first time, a yellow box says "First time here?". Tap "Fill in daycare details".

Fill in your name, address and phones. The office name (BC du Parc), division number (10586), rate ($9.65) and hours (9:30 to 14:30) are already filled in. Change them only if they are wrong.

Tap "Save settings". The yellow box disappears.

Later, when the rate or hours change, tap "Daycare settings" on the main screen and change it there.


STEP 3 - ADD A CHILD

Tap "+ New child".

Fill in the child, Parent A and Parent B. Phone numbers, postal codes and SIN numbers tidy themselves up when you move to the next box.

Parent B: "Same address and home phone as Parent A" is ticked already, so you only type their name, cell, email and SIN. If there is no second parent, tick "There is no second parent on the forms".

Care: tap the days the child comes. Times are copied from your opening hours. Pick the first day. "Agreement ends" fills itself in as August 31st.

Contribution form questions: the usual answers (Parent A signs, Canadian citizen, no social assistance, no other daycare) are already selected. Tap the other answer only if it is different.

The Ministère contract (5 pages) is off. Tick it only for the rare family that needs it.


STEP 4 - MAKE THE FORMS

Tap "Save and make forms".

A PDF named "Forms - (child's name).pdf" lands in your Downloads.

A message under the button tells you if anything important was left blank (for example, a missing SIN). It still makes the forms. The blank boxes just print empty.

Print it double-sided. Signatures and dates are left blank for signing.

To print the forms again later: on the main screen, tap "Make forms" next to the child.


STEP 5 - ATTENDANCE SHEETS (EVERY TWO WEEKS)

On the main screen, tap "Attendance sheets".

Pick the first Monday of the two weeks. If you pick a day that isn't a Monday, it tells you.

All current children are ticked. Untick anyone you don't want.

Tap "Make attendance sheets". One PDF downloads, one sheet per child.


STEP 6 - WHEN A CHILD LEAVES

On the main screen, tap "Child left" next to the child.

Pick their last day, and tap "Make Attestation".

The Attestation downloads (3 copies: parent, you, the office). The day counts are left blank, as usual.

The child moves to "Past children" at the bottom of the main screen. From there you can make the Attestation again, or tap "Still attending" if it was a mistake.


STEP 7 - SAVE A BACKUP (NOW AND THEN)

The children are saved in Chrome on this tablet only. If Chrome's data is ever cleared, they are gone.

On the main screen, tap "Save backup". A file named "Daycare backup (date).json" downloads. Put it somewhere private, for example in Google Drive. It holds SIN numbers.

To bring everything back, tap "Load backup" and pick that file.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG ON A PRINTED FORM

Take a screenshot of the wrong spot and send it. Each form's positions are set separately, so one form can be fixed without touching the others.


WHEN THE OFFICE SENDS A NEW VERSION OF A FORM

Send the new form. That one form gets set up again. The rest of the tool stays the same.
