🎧 Audio Finder

Finds audio links inside a Chrome network log (chrome://net-export) and saves them to your Downloads folder, one at a time.

How to make the log file
  1. In Chrome, open chrome://net-export and tap Start Logging to Disk.
  2. Go to the site and play (or start playing) each audio you want — this makes the links appear in the log.
  3. Come back to net-export and tap Stop Logging, then save the .json with "save to device" into your Downloads.
  4. Come back here and pick that file below.
Reading the log…

Files download one at a time with a pause in between, so the tablet stays happy. The first time, Chrome will ask permission to download multiple files — tap Allow.
