INSTALLING THE TOOLS AS THEIR OWN APPS

Each tool installs as its own app with its own icon and window.
The front page installs as an all-in-one launcher. About a minute
per app, on each device where you want it.

One thing first: icons added to the home screen BEFORE this
existed only open Chrome. Remove those (press and hold the icon,
then "Remove") and install fresh.


1. In Chrome, open the page you want as an app:

For the converter:
https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/bulktotxtconverter.html

For Audio Finder:
https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/audio-finder.html

For the all-in-one launcher, the front page:
https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/


2. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu, then "Install app". On some
versions it says "Add to Home screen" — same thing, tap it.


3. Tap "Install" (or "Add").


4. Find the new icon on your home screen: a yellow page for the
converter, teal headphones for Audio Finder, a yellow U for the
launcher. Tap it — the tool opens in its own window, no address
bar, with its own entry in recent apps. In DeX it gets its own
desktop-style window.


5. Repeat from step 1 for the next tool you want.


IF "INSTALL APP" DOES NOT APPEAR IN THE MENU: Chrome sometimes
needs a moment to notice a page became installable. Close the
tab, reopen the page, wait a few seconds, and check the menu
again.
