MAKING THE UTILITIES SITE OPEN IN ITS OWN WINDOW

Takes about 1 minute. Do it on each device where you want the
tools to feel like an app.

One thing first: if you added the site to your home screen BEFORE
today, that old shortcut only opens Chrome. Remove it and add it
fresh — the steps below cover both.


1. Press and hold the old Utilities icon on your home screen, then
choose "Remove". This removes only the shortcut — the site itself
is untouched.


2. Open https://moshgrossman.github.io/Utilities/ in Chrome.


3. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu and look for "Install app". On some
versions it says "Add to Home screen" instead — that is fine too.
Tap it.


4. Tap "Install" (or "Add").


5. Find the new icon on your home screen — a yellow U on black —
and tap it. The site opens in its own window: no address bar, no
Chrome tabs, and it shows up in your recent-apps list like any
other app. In DeX it gets its own desktop-style window.


IF IT STILL OPENS INSIDE CHROME: Chrome sometimes needs a minute
to notice a site became installable. Close the tab, reopen the
site, wait a few seconds, and try the menu again.
