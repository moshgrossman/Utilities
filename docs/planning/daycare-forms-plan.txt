HOW IT WORKS (YOU TYPE THE INFORMATION)

1. Open the tool in Chrome. It is a new page in your Utilities collection, next to Form Filler. [DEFAULT]

2. You see a list of your children and a big "New child" button.

3. Tap "New child". One screen opens, in English, in short sections:

   - Child: name, date of birth, sex

   - Parent A and Parent B: name, address, phones, email, SIN

   - Schedule: days, arrival/departure times, first day, end date

   - Contribution: reduced rate or exempt, Canadian citizen yes/no

4. Anything typed once is never asked twice. Example: Parent B's address is copied from Parent A, with one tap to change it.

5. Yes/no questions and choices are taps. The tool writes the French word on the form (Oui/Non, M/F, Lundi...).

6. Your daycare's details are already on every form: Gitty Roth, 755 Querbes, the phones, hours 9:30-2:30, $9.65, BC du Parc, division 10586. A "Daycare settings" page lets you change them when the rate or hours change. [DEFAULT]

7. Tap "Make forms". One PDF downloads.

   - The registration forms come first, in the order the office lists them, then the health form for the parents. [DEFAULT]

   - Wherever a form ends on a front page, a blank page is added, so each form starts on a fresh sheet when printed double-sided.

   - Signature and date lines are left blank.

8. Print double-sided, have it signed, scan it. Same as today, minus the writing.



ATTENDANCE SHEETS

9. On the main screen, tap "Attendance sheets".

10. Choose the two-week period. The "Semaine débutant le" dates fill in automatically.

11. One sheet per current child, with the coordinating office, child name, parent name and your name filled in. The day boxes and the payment section stay blank for you to write by hand.

12. [DEFAULT] One combined PDF for all the children, one sheet each.



WHEN A CHILD LEAVES

13. Tap the child, then "Child left", and enter the last day.

14. The tool makes the Attestation des services de garde fournis, with the parent and child details filled in.

Section 3 (number of days of care) is left blank, as you do today.

15. The end date also goes on that child's last attendance sheet, and the child moves to a "Past children" list. [DEFAULT]



WHAT IS SAVED, AND WHERE

16. The children's information is saved on the tablet only, in Chrome. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. That matters: these forms hold SIN numbers.

17. Because Chrome can lose saved data if its storage is cleared, a "Save backup" button downloads one file, and "Load backup" restores it. [DEFAULT]

18. "Delete child" really deletes.



WHEN THE OFFICE SENDS NEW VERSIONS

The tool knows exactly where each box sits on each form. If the office changes a form, that one form has to be set up again. That takes about one short session, not a new tool.

[DEFAULT] The tool shows the year of each form version on the settings page, so you can tell at a glance whether it matches what the office last sent.
